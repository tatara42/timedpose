Timed Pose
Overview

Timed Pose is a digital application designed to assist artists in practicing gesture drawing with a focus on time management. Whether you're a beginner looking to improve your skills or a seasoned artist honing your craft, Timed Pose provides a structured environment for timed gesture drawing sessions.

Features
    Customizable Timer: Set the duration for each pose session according to your preference, ranging from seconds to minutes.
    Varied Pose Selection: Choose from a library of diverse poses to suit your drawing practice needs.
    Pause and Resume: Pause the timer at any time during a session to take a break or adjust settings, then seamlessly resume where you left off.
    Feedback and Progress Tracking: Track your progress over time and receive feedback on your drawing sessions to help you improve.
    Export and Share: Save your drawings locally or share them with others to showcase your progress or receive critique.

How to Use
    Select Pose: Choose a pose from the available options in the library.
    Set Timer: Set the desired duration for your drawing session.
    Start Drawing: Begin your gesture drawing session. Focus on capturing the essence and movement of the pose within the time limit.
    Pause and Resume: Pause the timer if needed, then resume when ready to continue.
    Review and Export: Review your drawings, save them locally, or share them with others for feedback or inspiration.

Installation
    Timed Pose is available for download on Windows.
